The Opportunity

Living systems for worlds with limited resources.

Future settlements must minimize resupply, conserve water, recover nutrients, repurpose waste, and produce essentials locally.

Grow in place Useful materials from biological feedstocks.
Recover value Nutrients and resources from waste streams.
Build resilience Adaptable systems for constrained habitats.

The same principles benefit remote communities, resilient agriculture, and resource-limited operations here on Earth.

Research Directions

Investigating the potential of fungal systems for extraterrestrial habitats and resource-constrained environments.

Mycoarchitecture & Materials

Investigating grown composites for insulation, structural components, protective layers, and lower-mass habitat construction.

Mycelium composites Habitat materials Thermal insulation

Circular Resource Systems

Studying fungal roles in organic waste conversion, nutrient recovery, water treatment, and selective contaminant processing.

Waste valorization Mycofiltration Nutrient recovery

Regenerative Growing Systems

Developing concepts for compact fungal production, food-system integration, and biological nutrient cycling in controlled environments.

Closed-loop agriculture Cultivation Food systems

Extreme-Environment Biology

Exploring strain selection and stress tolerance for harsh conditions, including long-term concepts for regolith conditioning and habitat support.

Fungal genetics Environmental resilience Regolith concepts
